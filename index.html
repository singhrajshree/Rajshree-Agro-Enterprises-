How we work

A clear path from enquiry to shipment.

Each order is evaluated individually so that product specifications and commercial terms are understood before commitments are made.

01 / ENQUIRE

Share your requirement

Tell us the product, grade, quantity, packaging, destination port and target delivery period.

02 / SOURCE

Supplier coordination

We check sourcing possibilities and seek relevant product and packing details from suppliers.

03 / CONFIRM

Commercial proposal

We discuss price basis, Incoterms, payment, shipment schedule and required documents.

04 / SHIP

Export coordination

After agreement, we coordinate the agreed documentation and shipment arrangements.

International trade support

Practical coordination for cross-border trade.

We welcome enquiries for FOB, CFR and CIF quotations, subject to product suitability, route availability and final commercial agreement. The applicable Incoterms, shipment responsibilities and insurance arrangements will be stated in the quotation.
